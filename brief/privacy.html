Privacy Policy — Brief for Google Sheets

Last updated: 5 September 2026

Brief is a Google Sheets add-on that turns the spreadsheet you have open into a formatted report. This policy describes exactly what it can access, what it stores, and what leaves your browser.

Every statement below is a description of how the add-on is built, not a promise about intent. The three OAuth scopes it requests are what enforce it.

What Brief can access

Brief requests three OAuth scopes. Each is narrow, and together they are the smallest set that lets the add-on work:

Scope What it allows
spreadsheets.currentonly Read the one spreadsheet you currently have open, and only while the add-on is running
script.container.ui Show the report in a dialog inside that spreadsheet
script.external_request Make one outbound request, described below

Brief cannot access your Google Drive, your other spreadsheets, your Gmail, your Calendar, your contacts, or your identity. It does not request the scopes that would allow any of that. It does not know your name or email address: it never calls Google's user-identity APIs, and it holds no scope that would let it.

What Brief stores

Two values, in Google's own per-user storage for this add-on (PropertiesService.getUserProperties()), which lives in your Google account:

  • whether you hold a paid licence,
  • your licence key, if you entered one,
  • a count of how many free reports you have generated in the current calendar month.

That storage is scoped to you and to this add-on. The developer cannot read it, and it is not transmitted anywhere.

What leaves your browser

Your spreadsheet data never leaves your browser. The report is built inside the add-on and rendered in a dialog. It is not uploaded, not sent to a server the developer controls, not processed by any AI service, and not retained anywhere.

There is exactly one outbound request, and it happens only when you click Activate to enter a licence key. Brief sends to api.gumroad.com:

  • the licence key you typed,
  • the product identifier for Brief.

Nothing else. No spreadsheet content, no column names, no file name, no identifier for you. Gumroad's handling of that request is covered by Gumroad's own privacy policy.

If you never enter a licence key, Brief makes no outbound request at all.

What the developer receives

Nothing automatic. There is no analytics, no telemetry, no error reporting to the developer, no cookies, and no server operated by the developer.

If you purchase a licence, Gumroad — not Brief — processes that transaction and shares with the developer what any seller sees about a sale.

Removing your data

Uninstalling the add-on removes its access. The stored values described above live in your Google account and are removed by Google when the add-on is uninstalled. Because the developer never held that data, there is nothing to request deletion of.

Changes

Material changes to this policy will be reflected here with a new date. The add-on's scopes cannot change without Google prompting you to re-authorize, so any expansion of what Brief can access is something you would be asked to approve.
